Not only a vision

This sculpture already stands in Israel.

Before it was a concept, it was installed — on Tel Aviv's streets and beaches, marking the places where someone's presence is missing.

The idea

When someone leaves this world, they leave behind more than absence.

They leave behind moments. Relationships. Laughter. Love.

A whole life that continues living in memory.

From idea to project

This vision became part of Lezichram.

Lezichram is a memorial project created by Yagel Bar. It reimagines remembrance by focusing not only on loss, but on the life, memories, and human moments that remain.

What comes next

Now I want to bring these memories to life.

With AI, this idea can evolve into an immersive visual experience: a sculpture that becomes a living portal through memory, allowing people to move through someone's life, moment by moment.
